Privacy Policy

Last Updated: March 30, 2026

Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how we ("we," "us," or "our") handle information in connection with your use of our mobile application (the "App"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our practices.

Information We Collect

Information You Provide

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information that directly identifies you. The App does not require you to create an account or provide any personal details.

Automatically Collected Information

The App may collect certain non-personal information automatically, including:

  • Device type and operating system version
  • App usage statistics (e.g., features used, session duration)
  • Technical information necessary for the App to function properly

All data collected is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted to our servers or any third parties.

Local Data Storage

The App stores game progress, settings, and preferences locally on your device using standard device storage mechanisms. This information includes:

  • Game progress and scores
  • User preferences and settings
  • In-game achievements and statistics

This data remains on your device and is not accessible to us or any third parties. You can delete this data at any time by uninstalling the App or clearing the App's data through your device settings.

Third-Party Services

The App may use third-party services for advertising and analytics. These services may collect their own data according to their respective privacy policies.

Advertising

The App may display advertisements provided by Unity Ads. Unity Ads may collect information to provide personalized ads. The information collected may include:

  • Device identifiers (such as advertising ID)
  • IP address
  • Device type and operating system
  • Ad interaction data

Please review Unity Ads' privacy policy for more information: https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy

You can opt out of personalized advertising by adjusting your device's privacy settings (e.g., "Limit Ad Tracking" on iOS or "Opt out of Ads Personalization" on Android).

Analytics

We may use third-party analytics services to understand how the App is used. These services may collect anonymous usage data such as:

  • App opens and session duration
  • Feature usage patterns
  • Device and operating system information
  • Crash reports and performance data

This information is collected in aggregate form and does not identify individual users.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us, and we will take steps to delete such information.

Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect the information stored locally on your device. However, please be aware that no method of electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your Rights and Choices

You have the following rights regarding your information:

  • Access and Deletion: You can delete all locally stored data by uninstalling the App or clearing the App's data through your device settings.
  • Opt-Out of Personalized Ads: You can disable personalized advertising through your device's privacy settings.
  • Do Not Track: The App does not respond to "Do Not Track" signals, as we do not track users across different websites or apps.

International Data Transfers

As the App stores data locally on your device and does not transmit personal information to our servers, there are no international data transfers of your personal information.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this Privacy Policy. We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.

Your continued use of the App after any modifications to this Privacy Policy will constitute your acknowledgment of the modifications and your consent to abide by the modified Privacy Policy.

Data Retention

Since all data is stored locally on your device:

  • Data is retained until you delete the App or clear its data
  • We do not maintain copies of your data on our servers
  • Uninstalling the App will permanently delete all associated data from your device

Compliance

This Privacy Policy is designed to comply with:

  • General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)
  • California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA)
  • Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA)
  • Other applicable data protection laws and regulations

Your Consent

By using the App, you consent to this Privacy Policy.
